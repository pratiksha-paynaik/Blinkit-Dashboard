# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Title / Headline

**Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution across outlets, products, and locations.

---

## Purpose

The Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard is a business intelligence solution developed in Power BI to evaluate sales trends, customer ratings, product performance, and outlet characteristics. The dashboard provides actionable insights that support data-driven decision-making and business optimization.

---

## Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization
* 📂 **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI calculations and dynamic measures
* 🗄️ **SQL** – Data cleaning and standardization
* 📁 **File Formats** – `.pbix` and `.png`

### Skills Demonstrated

* Data Cleaning & Transformation
* SQL Querying
* Data Modeling
* DAX Calculations
* KPI Development
* Dashboard Design
* Business Analysis

---

## Data Source

**Source:** Blinkit Grocery Sales Dataset

The dataset contains transactional sales, product details, customer ratings, and outlet information across **8,500+ records**, enabling analysis of sales performance, inventory distribution, and customer preferences.

---

## Dashboard Overview

### Business Problem

Retail grocery businesses generate large volumes of sales data across multiple outlets and product categories, making it difficult to identify performance trends, customer preferences, and growth opportunities.

**Key Business Questions:**

* Which outlet types generate the highest revenue?
* Which product categories contribute the most sales?
* How does performance vary across outlet locations?
* What are customers' product preferences?
* Which outlet sizes perform best?

### Goal of the Dashboard

* Monitor overall sales performance.
* Analyze customer satisfaction through ratings.
* Evaluate product and inventory performance.
* Identify high-performing outlets and locations.
* Support data-driven business decisions.

### KPI Summary

| KPI              |  Value |
| ---------------- | -----: |
| Total Sales      | $1.20M |
| Average Sales    |   $141 |
| Total Items Sold |  8,523 |
| Average Rating   |    3.9 |

### Key Visuals

* **Outlet Establishment Analysis** – Sales trends by outlet establishment year.
* **Sales by Fat Content** – Comparison of Low Fat and Regular product sales.
* **Sales by Item Type** – Revenue contribution by product category.
* **Sales by Outlet Size** – Performance across outlet sizes.
* **Sales by Outlet Location** – Sales comparison across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 locations.
* **Sales by Outlet Type** – Analysis of sales, ratings, and item distribution by outlet type.
* **Interactive Filters** – Dynamic filtering by outlet location, size, and item type.

### Key Insights

* Supermarket Type 1 generated the highest revenue, contributing approximately **$787K** in sales.
* Tier 3 outlets recorded the highest sales contribution, generating approximately **$472K** in revenue.
* Low Fat products generated higher sales than Regular products.
* Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods were the top-performing product categories, generating approximately **$180K** each.
* Medium-sized outlets contributed the highest share of total revenue.

This dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights by analyzing sales trends, outlet performance, customer preferences, and product categories. The insights generated can help stakeholders optimize operations, improve decision-making, and identify opportunities for business growth.
